## Three-Phase Commercialization

| Phase | Name | Timeline | Description |
|-------|------|----------|-------------|
| Phase 1 | Cell-Free Construct | 18-24 months | Hydrogel + 4 signals only. Lower regulatory burden (510(k)/De Novo). Fastest path to market and revenue. |
| Phase 2 | Construct + Cells | 36-48 months | Add autologous or allogeneic cells. BLA pathway through CBER. Enhanced efficacy for severe cases. |
| Phase 3 | Vertical Integration | 48-60 months | In-house cell sourcing, manufacturing, and distribution. Maximum margins, supply chain control. |
