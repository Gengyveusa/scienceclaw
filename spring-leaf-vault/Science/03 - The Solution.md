# The Solution

> 4-Signal Minimal Viable Construct delivering the **complete regenerative signal**.

---

## The 4 Signals

| Signal | Full Name | Role |
|--------|-----------|------|
| **NGF** | Nerve Growth Factor | Neural ingrowth |
| **PDGF-BB** | Platelet-Derived Growth Factor | Vascular support |
| **BMP-7** | Bone Morphogenetic Protein 7 | PDL differentiation |
| **BDNF** | Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor | Neural survival |

Delivered in a **dual-crosslinked HA/alginate hydrogel**.
