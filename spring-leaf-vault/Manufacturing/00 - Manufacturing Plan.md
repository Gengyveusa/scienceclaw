# Manufacturing Plan

> Scaling strategy, vertical integration, domestic sourcing

**Website:** [spring-leaf-platform.vercel.app/manufacturing](https://spring-leaf-platform.vercel.app/manufacturing)

---

## Cost Reduction Trajectory

```
Phase 1: $180/unit  -->  Phase 2: $140/unit  -->  Phase 3: $85/unit
                                                   (53% reduction)
```

---

## Phase 1: Contract Manufacturing (Months 0-24)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Setup Costs | $200K-$300K |
| Unit Cost | $180 |
| Batch Size | 500 units |
| Annual Capacity | 10,000 units/year |

**Strategy:**
- Partner with FDA-registered CMOs experienced in hydrogel and biologics
- Technology transfer
- QA/QC specification establishment
- Inventory building for market launch

**Advantages:** Minimal capital investment, FDA-compliant facilities, flexible scaling, focus on R&D and commercialization

**Risks:** Supply chain dependence, lower margins, IP exposure, quality control distance

---

## Phase 2: Hybrid Model (Months 24-48)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Setup Costs | $1.5M-$2M |
| Unit Cost | $140 |
| Batch Size | 1,000 units |
| Annual Capacity | 50,000 units/year |

**Strategy:**
- Establish in-house quality laboratory
- Bring final formulation and packaging internally
- Maintain CMO for bulk manufacturing
- Develop cell processing capabilities

**Advantages:** 22% reduction in COGS, improved quality control, faster formulation iteration, developing cell handling expertise

**Risks:** Increased capital requirements, regulatory complexity from facility registration, staffing challenges

---

## Phase 3: Vertical Integration (Months 48+)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Setup Costs | $8M-$12M |
| Unit Cost | $85 |
| Batch Size | 5,000 units |
| Annual Capacity | 500,000 units/year |

**Strategy:**
- Dedicated manufacturing facility
- Domestic raw material sourcing
- In-house cell banking and processing
- Automated production lines

**Advantages:** Maximum gross margins (82%+), complete supply chain control, rapid demand response, full IP protection

**Risks:** Significant capital investment, facility qualification timelines, operational complexity

## Quality and Regulatory Framework

### Compliance Standards
- 21 CFR Part 820 (QSR)
- ISO 13485:2016
- cGMP for biologics (Phase 2+)

### Testing Requirements
- Sterility testing
- Endotoxin limits
- Growth factor potency assessment
- Rheological property evaluation

### Shelf Life
- **Target:** 24 months at 2-8 C
- Real-time stability studies
- Accelerated aging protocols

---

## Domestic Sourcing Strategy (Phase 3)

**Rationale:** Risk reduction, demand response speed, simplified regulatory documentation, quality audit accessibility

**Target Regions:**
- Northeast corridor - biomanufacturing
- Research Triangle - R&D
- California - component suppliers
