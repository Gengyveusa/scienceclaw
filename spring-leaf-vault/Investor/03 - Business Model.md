# Business Model

---

## Pricing and Margins

| Phase | ASP (per unit) | Gross Margin |
|-------|---------------|-------------|
| Phase 1 (Cell-Free) | **$850** | **79%** |
| Phase 2 (Construct + Cells) | **$2,400** | **73%** |

## Revenue Model

- Direct sales to periodontists and oral surgeons
- Hospital and clinic procurement
- Distributor partnerships for market penetration

## Three-Phase Product Strategy

1. **Phase 1:** Cell-free construct (hydrogel + 4 signals) - fastest to market
2. **Phase 2:** Construct + cells - premium product for severe cases
3. **Phase 3:** Vertical integration - maximum margin optimization
