## Elevator Pitch

Spring Leaf is the first periodontal regeneration platform built on the insight that **neural ingrowth precedes and coordinates tissue regeneration**. Current treatments produce structure without function - Spring Leaf restores proprioception and true tissue integration through a 4-signal construct (NGF, PDGF-BB, BMP-7, BDNF) in a dual-crosslinked HA/alginate hydrogel.

**First-mover in neural-first regeneration.**
